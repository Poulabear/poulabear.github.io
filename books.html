The Greenlandic Reconciliation Model In progress

Danish title: Den grønlandske forsoningsmodel

A practitioner's handbook on the reconciliation model that Poul developed and that the Government of Greenland adopted in 2013.

The book grew out of a simple observation: much Nordic academic work on reconciliation explains why it matters, but says little about how to actually do it. This handbook aims to fill that gap with concrete methods, processes and examples that people in municipalities, organisations and communities can use.

Planned for publication in Danish and English. Interested in early drafts or in using the model? Write to poul@nanu.dk.
