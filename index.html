Bio

Poul Bloch Jensen is a Danish-Greenlandic entrepreneur, educator and researcher based on Langeland, Denmark. He works through his holding company Nanu ApS, with a focus on trade between North America, Denmark and Greenland, on learning programmes for Greenlanders in Denmark, and on reconciliation after colonialism.

In 2013 he developed a reconciliation model that was adopted by the Government of Greenland (Naalakkersuisut). He has taught Action Learning-based innovation since 2010 and is working towards a PhD at Roskilde University. He speaks Danish, English and Greenlandic, and his work combines business that pays its own way with clear social value.

Alongside this, Poul has always been a hands-on technologist. He has built websites, CRM setups and automations for his own companies and for clients for many years, and today he uses AI agents and tools daily to run his ventures with a small team. In 1996 he was part of introducing the internet in Greenland and created the first ever Greenlandic hyphenation and spell checker, developed in collaboration with Microsoft for Microsoft Office. In 2008 he helped build Sermit, Greenland's joint public-sector IT centre. For 25 years he has also worked closely with Indian software developers.

Poul has also published 20 children's books in Greenlandic, translations of books by Robert Munsch, as a non-commercial project to give Greenlandic children books in their own language.

IT and AI in practice

Technology is not a side topic for Poul, it is how the work gets done. He runs his ventures on a mix of open and European-friendly tools, and uses AI agents for daily routines: prioritising tasks, drafting emails, researching markets, and keeping projects moving. He is interested in digital sovereignty and in showing small companies that they can run efficiently without the big cloud platforms.
