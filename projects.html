Injuria

A platform where people and businesses harmed by the same party can find each other, form a group and jointly fund investigation and legal action. Every case starts private and is vetted by a lawyer before anything is published.

injuria.eu
App · in development

Building condition app

A digital building survey. On a phone or tablet the surveyor records rooms, building parts, installations, photos, defects and condition grades 0 to 3. Back at the PC the same data becomes reports, prices and maintenance plans. It replaces paper-based surveys with a fast, structured and reusable workflow.
